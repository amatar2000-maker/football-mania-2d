Football Mania 2D – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Football Mania 2D ("the app") is an offline disc-football game. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal or device information. It does not request the Internet permission, has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDKs.

Data stored on your device

The game keeps only temporary game state (scores, selected team, settings) in your device's memory while you play. Nothing leaves your device.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Team names

Club names in the game are fictional. National team names and flags are used for identification only. The app is not affiliated with or endorsed by any football association, league or club.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
